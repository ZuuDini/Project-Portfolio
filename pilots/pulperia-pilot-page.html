How the pilot receptionist works

1

Only missed calls forward

Busy or no answer (after hours too, if you want), after 3 to 4 rings. Everything else stays with your host.

2

Sofia answers and says what she is

"Pulpería, this is Sofia, the AI host. Calls may be recorded." English or Spanish, switching mid-call.

3

Answers the common questions

Hours, location, menu and prices, brunch, pre-theater. Never promises a dish is allergen-free.

4

Takes a reservation request, not a booking

Date, time, party size, name, phone, then email, plus any occasion notes. She never offers, refuses or confirms a time: someone from the team gets back to the guest. Nothing goes into OpenTable or Resy. Your host enters every booking.

5

The request reaches one supervisor

Every finished call sends its request by text or email to the one person you name, within a minute of hang-up. The guest gets a text saying the request was received.

6

Anyone who asks for a person gets one

Live transfer to the number you give us, or a callback message if nobody picks up. No card numbers are taken. Complaints go straight to a manager.

Every call adds a row to your missed-call sheet: time, caller number, what they wanted.
